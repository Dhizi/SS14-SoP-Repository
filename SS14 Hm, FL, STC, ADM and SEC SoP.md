# Freelance Department SoP

### Harbor  Master
The HM abides by guidelines as follows:

 - The HMs primary task is to regulate the in-and-out flow of Freelancers aboard the station and Harbour by using delegation, while also providing Freelancers a reliable service, harbour-space and assuring that their rights are not infringed upon in any manner that is illegal. Through the means of communication, their staff, harbour upkeep, bounties and checkpoint moderation they are able to fulfill their duties to the station by sufficiently providing a reliable leader who can function as a strong communicator between Freelancer and station staff and security.
 - The HM is tasked with being responsible with the duty of procuring and deploying bounties  for the necessity of the Station. A bounty is: A contracted agreement to fulfill a specific, or list of, specific tasks. All bounties are to be tracked within any form of document, along with Contractor identification, payment, and a statement of failure/success upon completion.  Upon contract completion, an agreed-upon sum of Spesos (or specific bartered goods) is provided to the contractor in question. Once a contract is closed, the contractor is no longer tied to its responsibilities.
 -  The HM is granted powers equivalent to the Captain in regards to Freelancers and their staff. A HM may determine that any staff or Freelancer is an endangerment to their harbour, the station or other freelancers, they have the representative powers to strip or demote a Freelancer of their title, much as if the Captain deems a CO incapable of fulfilling their duties, they could do the same. If a demotion or title stripping is to occur, it is to be recorded in a document log clarifying the Freelancer, the reason, and the time of demotion or title removal. By default, a title strip/demotion may only persist for the shift of which it occurred in. The demotion document is to be then delivered at shift-end to any CC representative for filing. A demotion/title strip may be contested by the individual(s) in question if they follow proper procedure to reach out to a CC representative for final decision. Should a Freelancer resist enforcement of lawful title stripping/demotion, the HM is permitted free legal force to do as is necessary to fulfill this action. If resistance escalates to violence, the HM is permitted by choice, lethal or non-lethal force.
 - The HM may freely navigate between harbour and station space, but must always return to their duty space if called upon. A HM has required obligation to abide to duties in relation to Freelancers before all other -- Only a Captains order or higher may take precedence.
 - The HM defers power to all CC staff and greater. Should a decision be reached in relation to any Freelancer by a CC representative, the HM must lawfully abide by the decision. If they feel that a decision may be in error, they may fax or directly speak & contest the decision with the representative that made the declared choice. Should the decision be agreed upon by both parties, it is the HMs duty to complete this decisions required actions by all means of their power.
 - The HM holds the duty-bound right to forcefully demand the bluespace-logging (saving) of any vessel that has had its captain stripped of title/demoted.
